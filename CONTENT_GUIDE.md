## Content-writing rules

- Use first person in the About section.
- Make descriptions clear and specific.
- Prefer “I explored,” “I prototyped,” or “This project experiments with” when maturity is unclear.
- Distinguish concept, prototype, active development, deployed, and archived states.
- Say what problem the project addresses, what was implemented, and what was learned.
- Never infer project technology from a project name alone.
- Don't include stats unless they come from a stable real source and are necessary.
- Do not include private repository data, secret URLs, credentials, or sensitive information.
- Official contact email is `sushovanhalderofficial@gmail.com`.
- Do not pull Instagram into the professional header by default; social links should be relevant to the portfolio's purpose.

## Good example

“An experiment in parsing arithmetic expressions in a command-line calculator. The case study should explain the parser approach and supported operators after these have been verified from the repository.”

## Bad example

“An industry-leading calculator used by thousands of developers with a revolutionary parsing engine.”

The second example invents scale, quality, and impact.
